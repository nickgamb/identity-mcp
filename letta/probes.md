# Memory probes for tuning recall

Use these in a fresh conversation (no prior context in the thread) with the
Identity (Letta memory) model. Judge whether the agent:
- Finds the right memory without being asked to search
- Gets dates and context correct
- Answers naturally, not like a database lookup

## Probes (fill in specifics from your history after first ingest)

1. "What do you know about [a specific topic from your conversations]?"
2. "Do you remember when we talked about [a recurring theme]?"
3. "What are my main interests based on what you know about me?"
4. "Tell me about [a name or concept the agent should recognize]."
5. "What kinds of projects have we worked on together?"
